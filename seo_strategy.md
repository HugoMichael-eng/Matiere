# SEO Strategy

## In scope
- The public Sillage Lab / Matière landing page served by `artifacts/sillage-lab`.
- Search, social-preview, and AI-crawler visibility of public marketing content.
- Source-detectable accessibility issues on public pages that overlap with discoverability and usability.

## Out of scope
- Authenticated workspace routes (`/dashboard`, `/formulas/**`, `/materials`, `/files`, `/coach`, `/shop`).
- Mobile Expo app, design-system previews (`/s1/`, `/s2/`), API server, and mockup/prototyping tools unless they become public content surfaces.

## Target audience
- Independent perfumers and fragrance makers.

## Primary keywords
- Creative perfumery workspace
- Fragrance formula builder
- Perfumery materials and IFRA safety tracking
- AI coaching for independent perfumers

## Crawler assumptions
- The public landing page should be discoverable, indexable, shareable, and readable from initial HTML by search, social, and AI crawlers.
- Authenticated product workflows may remain client-rendered and are not marketing SEO pages.
